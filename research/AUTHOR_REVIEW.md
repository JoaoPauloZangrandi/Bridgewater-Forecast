The official terms state that the submission's substantive elements must be the
entrant's own independently conceived creative output and must not be
substantially AI generated. Approval of an AI-written draft alone does not make
that representation true. Bridgewater and Global Citizen retain sole
discretion over eligibility.

Before draft mode can be removed, Joao should provide all of the following in
his own words, without paraphrasing the manuscript:

1. A 300-500 word explanation of the central thesis and causal mechanism.
2. A Keep/Change decision and a personally chosen probability for F01-F12.
3. The three forecasts he has the strongest conviction in and why.
4. The strongest argument against the framework and which probabilities it
   would change.
5. At least two substantive passages he wants included in the final framework.
6. Confirmation that he checked the sources and can defend every forecast in a
   live Bridgewater roundtable.

The next revision should use that material as the intellectual spine, not as a
cosmetic sign-off. The final validator must pass without `--allow-draft`.
